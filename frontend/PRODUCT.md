# MarketMySelf — Product Context

## Product Purpose
MarketMySelf is a talent billboard platform where professionals own a pixel section to market themselves to recruiters and companies. It replaces passive job-board applying with active personal branding — limited spots, real visibility, direct discovery.

## Users
**Job seekers**: Professionals tired of ignored applications who want to be discovered rather than just apply. Technical, Marketing, and Finance roles. Ambitious, frustrated with the status quo, willing to invest effort in standing out.

**Companies / Recruiters**: Tech companies done with outdated hiring pipelines who want to discover candidates by browsing a curated, high-intent talent billboard.

## Brand Voice
Defiant, direct, personal. Anti-corporate. Founder-led. Speaks to the frustration of invisible candidates and offers a way out. Not SaaS-cheerful, not startup-hype. Raw and honest.

## Register
brand

## Design Aesthetic
Dark void (#060810 background). Monospace + geometric sans type system. Three category accent colors: cyan (#22d3ee / Technical), purple (#c084fc / Marketing), green (#34d399 / Finance). Amber (#f59e0b) for urgency, warnings, and founder voice. Minimal surfaces — space is earned, not filled.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing pages, LinkedIn aesthetics, job board blue-and-white, startup stock photography, hero-metric dashboards, rounded icon grids.

## Strategic Principles
- Scarcity creates value (limited spots per page)
- Transparency builds trust (show who gets hired)
- Marketing > applying (profile-first, not resume-first)
- Timing matters (hiring countdowns)
